B · Entry flow

Three steps from arrival to immersion.

The home screen does one job: pull the visitor off the menu and onto the spine. Depth is opt-in, so no one is ever lost.

STEP 01

Arrive in the dark

A darkened stage. One light finds a silhouette. The title resolves; a single “enter” invites the spine.

STEP 02

Choose a way in

Follow the linear spine, or pick a pole — Isabela's Inner Flow or Diogo's Kinetic Boxes — straight from the portal.

STEP 03

Read at your own pace

Each stop offers opt-in panels. The chapter menu stays visible; sound from the piece carries across screens.
