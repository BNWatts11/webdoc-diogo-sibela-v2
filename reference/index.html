Sibela & Digoo

An interactive web documentary on inclusive dance — the Diogo & Sibela show by choreographer Tânia Carvalho, told through the dancers and the objects left on stage. Five planning documents, in order.

What this package is

The planning behind an interactive web documentary that moves the focus off the choreographer and onto the dancers — Isabela and Diogo — and the scenic objects their solos leave behind: the cardboard boxes and the chair.

Each document is standalone and printable, and they cross-link through the bar at the top of every page.

Built in

Accessibility runs through all of it — captions, transcripts, audio description, keyboard navigation — fitting for a project about inclusion. The visual language stays quiet and sound-led, honouring Tânia Carvalho's minimalism.
